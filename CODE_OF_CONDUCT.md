# Code of conduct

<p align="center">English | <a href="CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md">中文</a></p>

VBOT Lab welcomes participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed
collaboration spaces. The forum also has its own
[community guidelines](https://forum.vbot.cn/guidelines).

## Work together respectfully

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; avoid personal attacks, harassment and discrimination.
- Respect other people's time, experience, privacy and intellectual property.
- Keep discussions relevant. Do not spam, threaten others or publish private information without permission.
- Accept reasonable feedback and help newcomers find the relevant documentation.
- Put physical safety first. Do not pressure anyone to repeat an unsafe robot operation
  or bypass safety controls to demonstrate a problem.

## Report a concern

Contact the repository administrators privately at [vbot-edu@vbot.cn](mailto:vbot-edu@vbot.cn).
Describe what happened and include only relevant, sanitized evidence. Do not publish
personal information, private correspondence or credentials in a report.
Vulnerability reports should follow [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer response

The repository administrators handle reports, consider the context and impact, and
limit access to report details to people needed to respond. They may request changes,
remove inappropriate content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation
in project-managed spaces. Reporters and affected participants can ask the same
contact for clarification or reconsideration. A maintainer involved in a report
should not decide its outcome.
